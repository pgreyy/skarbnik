What happened

From a fix to a proven payment

  1. Bounty created and funded

    The vendor locked the reward. Every hash, the windows, K and the check size were fixed on chain. The bounty starts closed.

    create_bounty 8vAT8wZQ…LL6J

  2. Opened by Proof 0

    The vendor proved its own reference fix passes the full check. The contract verified that Groth16 proof itself, so the bounty could open.

    open_with_groth16 ErytPEKY…LnQM

  3. An attempt commits a fingerprint

    Attempt committed. Only a fingerprint of the patch goes on chain, with the attempt fee from credit.

    commit 86dYS838…Uyhg

  4. The attempt reveals its patch

    Attempt revealed. The patch became public on chain. The block seed chose the hidden fairness inputs, and the verdict window started.

    reveal HCjck1DL…QN4L

  5. A proof of the attempt was accepted

    Proof of an attempt sent. The contract checked the Groth16 proof itself and applied its verdict.

    submit_groth16 9B7FPyju…pM7y

Rules and timing

Rules and timing

The rules of this bounty

A 1 day verdict window

It starts at the reveal. A proof for the attempt must reach the contract before it ends.

Late proofs are refused

After the window the contract answers “verdict window has closed”.

Anyone can claim

claim_timeout splits the bounty equally among revealed attempts still undecided.

The fix is never read

A timeout pays whatever was revealed, even junk. That is why it is never called proof.
