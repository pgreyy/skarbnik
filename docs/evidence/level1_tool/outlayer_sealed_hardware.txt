Does OutLayer run our Level 1 checks inside sealed hardware? What was found, 2026-11-03.

OutLayer's own documentation says so:
- https://app.outlayer.ai/docs/tee-attestation (was outlayer.fastnear.com/docs/tee-attestation): workers run in Intel
  TDX; a worker registers on worker.outlayer.testnet (worker.outlayer.near on mainnet) with register_worker, which
  checks its TDX quote and five measurements (MRTD, RTMR0 to RTMR3); is_measurements_approved answers whether they are
  approved; only the worker's registered key may call resolve_execution; each execution's attestation (WASM hash,
  input and output hashes, measurement, TDX quote) is kept in OutLayer's coordinator database and shown in its
  dashboard; the page does not say it is returned with the result.
- https://app.outlayer.ai/docs/trust-verification: both kinds of worker (self hosted and Phala Cloud) are said to be
  Intel TDX; results are said to be signed with a TEE generated key registered on chain; resolve_execution,
  submit_execution_output_and_resolve, resume_topup and resume_delete_payment_key are scoped to worker access keys; an
  outside party can check an execution in the dashboard or with the outlayer-verify command line tool.

What the chain and our jobs show (read 2026-11-03, view_access_key_list on https://test.rpc.fastnear.com):
- worker.outlayer.testnet has 41 access keys: 38 function call keys limited to resolve_execution and
  submit_execution_output_and_resolve (35 of them also resume_topup and resume_delete_payment_key), and 3 FULL ACCESS
  keys. A full access key can do anything on that account; who holds those 3 keys, and whether they live in an
  enclave, is not shown on chain.
- outlayer.testnet has 1 full access key.
- The answers our contract received (execution_completed events, e.g. tx 3ZpV6zsgY8vuTCvZDSHv3E6NuvC6YgXTvtEQkvuqw92u)
  carry these fields only: code_source, compilation_note, error_message, payment_charged, payment_refunded,
  resources_used, sender_id, success, timestamp. No quote, no measurement, no attestation.
- Our contract does not check any attestation: it accepts the answer because it arrives in its own callback from
  outlayer.testnet (docs/SECURITY.md, Level 1 guards).

Verdict: sealed hardware for our checks is CLAIMED by OutLayer's docs and NOT SHOWN by us. We did not open the
dashboard attestation of any of our executions and did not run outlayer-verify. No page or doc of this project says
our Level 1 checks run in sealed hardware; docs/SECURITY.md and docs/CLAIMS.md say Level 1 trusts OutLayer, its
operator and its keys.
